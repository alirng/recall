**Upgrading turns the subagent brief on for every subagent, with no new question.** The Claude Code and Cursor hook entry that carries it was already installed under the explore consent you gave earlier, so the first subagent you start after upgrading gets about 1,000 tokens of recall (rules in force, earlier chats, open threads) added to its prompt, whatever its type. Codex is the exception: it needs `recall install` and a trust in `/hooks` first. To keep subagents as they were, set `subagents = false` under `[brief]` in config.toml before you upgrade.
