**One chat recall cannot index no longer breaks its tools.** A chat that failed to index made every tool call fail and kept the chats after it out of the index; a damaged archive copy did the same. Such a chat is now logged by its path, never its words, tried again at the next two runs, and then left until its file changes, while the others index as usual. On Linux, run `recall index --source cursor --force` once: Cursor chats without a record in Cursor's database failed to index and were not tried again.
