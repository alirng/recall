**One chat recall cannot index no longer breaks its tools.** A chat that failed to index made every tool call fail and kept the chats after it out of the index, and a damaged archive copy did the same. Such a chat is now logged by its path, never its words, and tried again after a minute, ten minutes and an hour, then left until its file changes, while the others index as usual. `recall doctor` lists these chats and `recall stats` counts them. A damaged archive copy is replaced by the next read of its file, and can no longer stop recall from opening the index after an update.
