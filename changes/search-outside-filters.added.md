**Search shows what a wrong filter hides.** When `search_chats` is given an agent, project or date filter, it also ranks without them, and the chats outside the filters that rank above every chat inside them, two at most, follow the results with their best hit and the filters they fail (for example `outside agent=cursor`). A wrong guess at the tool, the project or a since date no longer hides the right chat without a trace. When the filters keep the best match, the results are as before.
